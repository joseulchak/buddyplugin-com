Buddy Zoom

Buddy Zoom adds automatic zooms to keep talking-head videos moving. Zooms are Scale and Position keyframes on the Motion effect, so they stay editable. Nothing is cut or deleted, and the duration does not change.

1. Triggers

  • Target track (the top one): zooms apply to this track and every track below it. Keep lower thirds, graphics and b-roll on a track above so they stay still.
  • Ignore cuts on these tracks: cuts on the checked tracks do not start zooms, but their clips still follow every zoom.
  • Zoom pace: from Calm to Intense (default Medium). More intense = zooms that come more often, move faster and go a bit further.
  • Voice track (A1 by default): used by the speech and emphasis triggers.
Trigger Default When it starts a zoom
Cut Always on At the cuts of the timeline.
Speech On When speech starts again after a pause.
Voice emphasis Off When the voice gets louder than usual (a punchline, an exclamation). Uses the maximum zoom.

Fine tuning (advanced) holds Pause before the speech (default 0.6 s), Emphasis sensitivity (default +6 dB) and the Voice level, auto-calibrated on every analysis.

2. Framing

  • Click the frame preview to set the anchor point, the point that stays still while zooming. The dashed box shows what stays on screen at the maximum zoom.
  • Center and Face (upper third) are shortcuts for the anchor point.
  • Minimum zoom and Maximum zoom range from 101% to 200% (default 110% and 130%).

3. Style

Style What it looks like · good for
Jump cut Instant zoom, no animation. YouTube, commentary and high-energy edits.
Smooth Eased zoom in and out. Interviews, lessons and storytelling.
Snap-in Fast animated zoom in, instant zoom out. Podcasts, business and social videos.

Check one or more; Buddy mixes the checked styles. Cuts lean to Jump cut and voice emphasis uses Snap-in.

Subtle handheld movement (off) adds a small drift while zoomed in, like a handheld camera, without ever showing the edges of the image.

Analyze and apply

  1. Click Analyze timeline. The preview shows each zoom colored by style, the time zoomed in and the triggers found.
  2. Use Generate another variation if you want another version.
  3. Click Apply zooms to timeline.

Clips that get no zoom

Clips that already had Scale or Position keyframes are left as they are. Clips without the Motion effect, with speed other than 100% or reversed are skipped too. Without FFmpeg, only the Cut trigger works and the frame preview stays neutral.
