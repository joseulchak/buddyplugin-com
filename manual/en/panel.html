The panel

The Buddy panel is under Window > Extensions > Buddy Editor and can be docked like any Premiere panel.

Parts of the panel

  • Top: active sequence name, frame rate and duration. The circular arrows button refreshes the active sequence. The gear opens Settings.
  • Tabs: Podcast, Silences, Zoom and Angles.
  • Banners: appear under the tabs when an update is available, while Buddy waits for Premiere, or when something goes wrong (with the Send diagnostics button).
  • Footer: Premiere version, whether an NVIDIA (CUDA) graphics card was detected, and the Buddy version.

When to click refresh

Click the refresh button at the top whenever you switch sequences, add or remove tracks, or change the In and Out marks.

Language

Buddy follows Premiere's language: Portuguese when Premiere is in Portuguese, English otherwise. To change it, open Settings and pick the Language. The change applies immediately.
