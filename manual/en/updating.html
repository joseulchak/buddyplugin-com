Updating and uninstalling

Updating

When a new version is out, a banner appears at the top of the panel.

  1. Click Update. Buddy downloads the new version and checks its signature.
  2. If Windows asks for permission, click Yes.
  3. Close and reopen Premiere. Your settings are kept.

You can also look for new versions in Settings > Updates > Check for updates. The list of versions is in Version history.

If the permission is declined

The .zxp file is saved to your Downloads folder. To install it: close Premiere, open the ZXP Installer and drag the file into it.

Uninstalling

  1. If you will use the license on another computer, click Deactivate this computer in Settings > License.
  2. Close Premiere.
  3. Open the ZXP Installer and remove Buddy Editor.

The data folder %LOCALAPPDATA%\BuddyEditor (settings and FFmpeg) is kept. Delete it manually if you want.
