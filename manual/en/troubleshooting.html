Troubleshooting

Find the message or situation in the tables. If nothing helps, use Send diagnostics.

Installation and connection

Situation What to do
The panel is missing from Window > Extensions Install again with the ZXP Installer while Premiere is closed. If it persists, restart the computer.
"Waiting for Premiere to respond…" Normal while the project opens. It can take up to a minute.
"Premiere did not respond to Buddy" Wait for the project to finish opening and click refresh. If it persists, close the Adobe Stock panel (Window > Adobe Stock), restart Premiere and send diagnostics.
"Open a sequence in Premiere Pro" Open the sequence on the timeline and click refresh at the top of the panel.

License

Situation What to do
Tab with a lock Your license does not include that module. Start the free trial or activate a key.
"Needs online check" Buddy was offline for 7 days. Connect and reopen the panel.
"This license is already active on the maximum number of computers" Deactivate it on another computer first, in Settings > License.
"Clock changed" Fix the Windows date and time and reopen the panel.

Analysis and cuts

Situation What to do
Little speech detected or "No audio found" Check the mic or voice track is right (A1, A2…). For a multichannel file, keep the channel on Automatic.
Mics receiving the same audio Use the Split channels automatically button in the Podcast preview.
Cuts or switches in the middle of speech Open Fine tuning and lower the silence gate. In Silences, lower the Silence level.
"The sequence changed since the analysis" Click Analyze again before applying.
"This Premiere version cannot unlink by script" Select the clips, use Clip > Unlink (Ctrl+L) and apply again.
Some cuts could not be confirmed Check the timeline at that point. If something is wrong, go back with the backup copy.
Error in Linear mode If Premiere does not create the new track, nothing is cut. If it cannot move the pieces, the cuts were already made: go back with the backup copy.
Silences: "Premiere could not close the gap on every track" An upper track has a gap under a silence. Go back with the backup copy and run Silences before adding b-roll and lettering.
Zoom: clips skipped Clips that already had Scale or Position keyframes, have no Motion effect, or have speed other than 100% get no zoom.

Updating

Situation What to do
Windows asked for permission and I declined The .zxp file was saved to your Downloads folder. Close Premiere and install it with the ZXP Installer, or click Update again and accept.
"The download failed" Check your internet and try again.
