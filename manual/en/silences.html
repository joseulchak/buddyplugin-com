Buddy Silences

Buddy Silences removes the pauses from a talking-head video. It finds the silences on one audio track and cuts every unlocked track at the same points, so everything stays in sync. It is free.

Use it first

Run Silences as the first step of the edit, before music, b-roll or lettering. See why in B-roll and lettering.

1. Audio

  • Analyzed track: the voice track, A1 by default.
  • Silence level (default −38 dB): anything below it counts as silence. Raise it if room noise is not detected; lower it if soft words are being cut.
  • Range: Entire sequence or Sequence In/Out.
  • Locked tracks stay out of the edit. If the analyzed track is locked, it goes out of sync with the rest.

2. Rules

Option What it does
Shortest silence to removeDefault: 300 ms Shorter pauses stay in the video.
Keep after the speechDefault: 100 ms Room left at the end of a sentence, before the cut.
Keep before the speechDefault: 100 ms Room left before the next sentence starts, after the cut.
Remove short sounds between silencesDefault: on · up to 150 ms An "uh", click or lip noise alone in the middle of a pause is removed with it.
What to do with the silencesDefault: Cut and ripple delete Cut and ripple delete: removes them and closes the gaps; the video gets shorter.
Cut and delete: removes them and leaves the gaps.
Cut only: only cuts, deleting nothing, so you can review.
Audio fade at each cutDefault: off · 66 ms A short audio-only fade so cuts do not pop. The video stays a hard cut. The value is rounded to whole frames.

Analyze and apply

  1. Click Analyze audio. On the preview bar, blue stays and yellow is removed.
  2. Check the number of silences, the time removed, the duration now and the duration after.
  3. Click Apply cuts to timeline.

B-roll and lettering

A track with a gap under a silence can keep the gap from closing on every track. When that happens Buddy stops the edit to keep everything in sync and shows how many silences were already removed. Go back with the backup copy and run Silences before building the upper tracks.

Linked clips

If Premiere does not cut clips with linked audio and video, Buddy unlinks them by itself, tries again and says so in the result message.
