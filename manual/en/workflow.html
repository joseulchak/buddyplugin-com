Analyze, preview, apply

All four modules follow the same steps. Nothing changes on the timeline until the last click.

The workflow

  1. Configure. Open the sequence in Premiere and the module's tab. Buddy lists only the tracks that have clips and fills in default values. The setup is saved automatically for each sequence, and the last one used carries over to new sequences.
  2. Analyze. Click Analyze audio (in Zoom, Analyze timeline). Buddy reads the audio straight from the original files: it renders nothing and does not change the timeline. You can stop it with Cancel.
  3. Check the preview. A bar shows the whole timeline, with counts and warnings. Changing a rule updates the preview instantly, without analyzing again.
  4. Apply. Click the blue button. Buddy checks the sequence has not changed, creates the backup, edits and shows the result with the backup name.

If the sequence changed after the analysis (another active sequence, a different duration or new In and Out marks), Buddy asks you to analyze again.

Backup copy

Before touching the timeline, Buddy duplicates the sequence as <name>_BuddyBackup_<date_time>. The result message shows that name.

To go back

Open the _BuddyBackup_ sequence in the Project panel. It is an exact copy from before the edit.

Recurring controls

  • Fine tuning (advanced) reveals the manual controls. You rarely need it: Buddy calibrates itself on every analysis.
  • Exact values: double-click a number, type and press Enter (Esc cancels). Comma or dot both work.
  • Generate another variation (Zoom and Angles) draws a new version. The preview and the applied edit are always identical.
  • Range (Silences and Zoom): Entire sequence or Sequence In/Out, using Premiere's I and O marks.

Clips Buddy skips

During analysis Buddy skips the clips below, and the preview reports how many:

  • disabled clips;
  • clips with speed other than 100%, or reversed;
  • nested sequences and multicam;
  • items without a media file (generated inside Premiere).
