What Buddy Editor is

Buddy Editor is an extension for Adobe Premiere Pro on Windows that automates repetitive editing tasks. It reads the audio straight from the original files, shows a preview of what it will do, and only touches the timeline when you click apply.

Modules

Each module lives in its own tab of the panel.

Module (tab) What it does
Buddy PodcastPodcast tab Cuts and switches the cameras of a podcast based on who is speaking.
Buddy SilencesSilences tab · free Removes the pauses from a talking-head video, on every track at once.
Buddy ZoomZoom tab Adds automatic zooms on cuts, speech and voice emphasis.
Buddy AnglesAngles tab Switches the camera angles of one speaker, only at the pauses in the speech.

Principles

  • Nothing changes until you click. Analysis only reads the audio; the timeline changes only when you click apply.
  • There is always a backup. Before editing, Buddy duplicates the sequence. See Backup copy.
  • Nothing is rendered. Buddy reads the audio from the media files, so analysis is fast and runs on your computer.
  • The result stays editable. Cuts, disabled clips and keyframes sit on the timeline as if you had made them by hand.

Requirements

  • Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). macOS is not supported yet.
  • Adobe Premiere Pro 2022 (version 22) or newer.
  • The free aescripts ZXP Installer, to install the plugin.
  • Internet to activate the license and receive updates. Once activated, Buddy works for up to 7 days offline.
  • Nothing else to install: the package already includes FFmpeg, a free program (LGPL license) that Buddy uses to read audio.
