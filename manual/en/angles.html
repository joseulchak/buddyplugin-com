Buddy Angles

Buddy Angles is for videos of one person filmed with several cameras (wide, close-up, side…). It switches angles in random order, only at the pauses in the speech, so a word is never cut. The sequence duration does not change.

1. Angles

  • Voice track: the track with the person's audio. Buddy finds the pauses on it.
  • Check the video tracks that are angles of the same person (at least two) and name each one. Without a name they become "Angle 01", "Angle 02"…
  • Main camera: by default all angles are equal. If you pick a main camera, it comes back on air after every other angle and stays on screen longer.
  • Fine tuning (advanced) holds the channel, the Silence gate (auto-calibrated) and Gate above the noise (default 8 dB).

2. Rules

Option What it does
Micro pauseDefault: 0.12 s Shortest silence between words that can take a switch.
End of sentenceDefault: 0.40 s Silences at least this long count as an end of sentence.
Prefer the end of a sentenceDefault: on Uses an end of sentence first; a micro pause only when there is none.
Minimum shotDefault: 3.0 s No angle stays on air for less than this.
Maximum shotDefault: 10.0 s Switches at a pause near a random time in this range. Without a pause, it waits for the next one.

The edit options are the same as in Buddy Podcast, with the same defaults: Edit mode, Camera layers, Where to cut, Unlink audio and video, Also cut the voice audio, Inactive cameras and Empty tracks after editing.

Analyze and apply

  1. Click Analyze audio. The preview shows each angle in its own color, the time of each, the ends of sentence and micro pauses found, and the average shot.
  2. Use Generate another variation to draw a new order and new shot lengths, always at pauses.
  3. Click Apply cuts to timeline.

Switching in the middle of speech?

Open Fine tuning (advanced) and lower the Silence gate. If no pauses are found, raise the gate or lower the micro pause.
