Activation and license

Buddy Silences is free and works without activation. The other modules need the free trial or a license.

Activating Buddy Editor

When the panel opens without a license, the Activate Buddy Editor screen offers two options:

  • Start 7-day free trial: no card needed, all modules unlocked. One trial per computer.
  • License key: paste the BUDDY-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX key from your email and click Activate. Spaces and lowercase are accepted.

Computers

Each license covers 2 computers (for example, a desktop and a laptop). To use it on another computer, free a slot in Settings > License > Deactivate this computer and activate on the new computer.

Offline use

Buddy checks the license online every time the panel opens. Without internet it keeps working for up to 7 days. After that, the paid modules lock until the next check: connect and reopen the panel.

Locked tabs

A tab with a yellow lock is a module your license does not include. To unlock it, start the free trial or activate a key that includes that module.

License status

Status What it means
Active All good.
Not activated Buddy has not been activated on this computer yet.
Expired The subscription or trial has ended.
Needs online check Buddy could not check the license for 7 days.
Other computer The license was activated on another computer. Activate it here with your key.
Clock changed The computer's date and time were changed. Fix the clock and reopen the panel.
