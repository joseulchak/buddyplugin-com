Buddy Podcast

Buddy Podcast edits podcasts recorded with several cameras and one microphone per person. It listens to the mics, works out who is speaking at each moment and puts that person's camera on air.

  • Whoever is loudest on their own mic wins. A person's voice leaking into the other mics is always quieter, so it is ignored.
  • The sequence duration does not change. Buddy cuts and turns camera pieces on or off, but never pulls the timeline (no ripple delete).

Preparing the sequence

  • One microphone per audio track (A1, A2, A3…). If several mics share one multichannel file and each channel sits on its own track, Buddy splits the channels by itself, in the order Premiere shows them (Ch. 1, Ch. 2…).
  • One camera per video track (V1, V2, V3…).
  • Everything in sync before using Buddy.

1. Speakers

  • Buddy creates a speaker for each audio track with clips. Type each person's name and check the Mic track (A1, A2…).
  • The checkbox next to the name includes or ignores that mic. Add speaker creates one more.
  • Fine tuning (advanced) shows each mic's Silence gate, the Auto-calibrate button and the channel choice. Leave the channel on Automatic (same as Premiere).

2. Cameras

  • For each video track, mark whose camera it is. One person: Solo camera. Several people: Group shot. Nobody: Not used (Buddy leaves that track alone).
  • A person can have more than one camera (for example, a close-up and a medium shot). With Maximum shot on, Buddy alternates between them during long turns.
  • The Cameras linked summary warns when a speaker has no camera.

Wide shot

Mark the wide camera for all speakers. It becomes a group shot: it goes on air when two people talk together and alternates with the speaker's camera during long turns.

3. Rules

The defaults work for most podcasts.

Option What it does
Minimum shotDefault: 2.0 s Turns shorter than this do not switch cameras. Avoids choppy cuts on "uh-huh" and short laughs.
Maximum shotDefault: 6.0 s · can be turned off During long turns, switches to another camera marked for the same person (another angle or the wide shot) at each interval.
Pause toleranceDefault: 1.5 s Pauses shorter than this keep the current speaker on air.
Edit modeDefault: Cut and switch cameras Cut and switch cameras: keeps only the speaker's camera on air.
Cut only (keep all visible): only cuts at speaker changes, so you choose the cameras yourself.
Camera layersDefault: Stack Stack: only the camera on air stays on; each camera stays on its own track.
Linear: moves every piece that goes on air, in order, to a new video track on top.
Where to cutDefault: Only tracks that change Only tracks that change: each switch cuts only the camera leaving and the one entering.
All tracks: each switch cuts every camera track.
Unlink audio and video before cuttingDefault: on (recommended) Makes sure cutting a camera never cuts its audio too.
Also cut the mic audioDefault: off When on, the mic tracks are also cut at every switch.
Inactive camerasDefault: Disable clips Disable clips: pieces off air stay on the timeline, disabled (you can re-enable them).
Delete clips (lift): deletes those pieces and leaves a gap.
Inactive microphonesDefault: Keep audio intact Mute other mics while someone speaks: while one person speaks alone, the other mics are cut and disabled, removing bleed and noise.
Speaker markersDefault: off Adds a sequence marker at every switch, with the person's name.
Empty tracks after editingDefault: Keep all Can delete the empty video and/or audio tracks of the sequence, including ones that were already empty.

Analyze and apply

  1. Click Analyze audio. The preview shows the timeline colored by the camera on air, the number of cuts, each person's speaking time, cross-talk and silence.
  2. Adjust the rules if you want. The preview updates instantly.
  3. Click Apply cuts to timeline.

Preview warnings

Warning What to do
Mics receiving the same audio Use the Split channels automatically button in the preview.
Little speech detected Check in 1. Speakers that each person is on the right track, or open Fine tuning and lower the silence gate.
No audio found on a mic Check the mic track is right (A1, A2…).
Speaker without a camera Mark a camera for them in 2. Cameras. Meanwhile, the previous camera stays on air.
